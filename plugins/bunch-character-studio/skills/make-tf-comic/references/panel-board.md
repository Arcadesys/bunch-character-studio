# Transformation panel board

Brief: starting subject → final character; mechanism; tone; layout; style;
wardrobe; exact dialogue; fixed-photo or drawn-scene continuity.

| Panel | Visible state / new change | Traits held from prior panels | Action and reaction | Framing / prop contact | Exact text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Starting appearance; establish baseline | Identity anchors and wardrobe | Specific ordinary action | Establish setting and prop positions | Supplied text or none |
| 2 | First visible change | Unchanged features and established anchors | Notice the change through action | Keep geography legible | Supplied text or none |
| 3 | Unmistakable midpoint; named partial anatomy | All completed changes from panel 2 | Adapt to the changing body | Verify grip, limb count, clothing, attachment | Supplied text or none |
| 4 | Complete final design | Earlier changes, final profile, setting | Pay off the opening action | Readable final silhouette | Supplied text or none |

Expand or compress this structure to the requested count. Do not impose this
four-panel template on a user-specified sequence. Use concrete visual states,
not percentages alone: “fox ears replace human ears; muzzle still human” is
actionable; “25% fox” is not.

Per-panel prompt:

```text
Panel <ID> of <count>. <style and layout constraints>.
References: <each image's specific role>.
Starting subject and final destination: <identity anchors>.
Current state: <only features visible at this stage>.
New change: <the transformation beat>.
Hold: <already changed traits and unchanged scene details>.
Action: <specific behavior and physical contact>.
Camera and composition: <framing, screen direction, text space>.
Exact lettering: <text or no lettering>.
Avoid: premature final form, reverted traits, duplicate anatomy, wardrobe or
prop drift, extra characters, and edits to other panels.
```

Review: Can the sequence be understood without captions? Is it unmistakably the
same subject? Does the final form match the chosen reference? Are text and
reading order clear? Does every intermediate body have coherent anatomy?
